The Doubling Circuit

Two rings, both numbered 1 through 9. On each, a point of light jumps between six of those numbers on repeat — never touching 3, 6, or 9.

Ring A (blue) runs 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 7 → 5 → 1. Ring B (red) runs it backward: 1 → 5 → 7 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1.

Why 3, 6, and 9 stay dark: the path is what you get from repeatedly doubling 1 and reducing to a single digit (doubling mod 9) — 1, 2, 4, 8, 16→7, 32→5, 64→1 — and that loop never lands on a multiple of 3. Ring B just walks the same six numbers the other way.
